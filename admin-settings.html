How protected is this admin?

Honest answer: it keeps casual visitors out, but it is not server-level security.

  • Your store is made of plain files with no server, so the password check runs inside the visitor's browser. A technically skilled person could get past it.
  • Nothing private is exposed by that: this admin only sees data stored in your own browser. It can't read other people's orders, and it can't change your live store by itself.
  • To truly enforce "only me", turn on password protection for the admin pages at your hosting provider, or move to a hosting setup with real accounts.
